Our Story

Azeta is a tree to bar chocolate brand born in the serene cacao growing region of Pollachi, Tamil Nadu. Founded by Vibhu Nishanth, a chemical engineer turned chocolate craftsman. Azeta is where scientific precision meets agricultural heritage. Inspired by his family’s cacao plantation, Vibhu set out to build a brand that controls chocolate at its origin, believing that true flavour is created long before it reaches the factory.

Working closely with single origin cacao from Pollachi, Azeta follows traditional wooden box fermentation and in-house processing, shaping flavour at the source. By combining time honoured practices with modern machinery and strict process control, Azeta delivers chocolate that balances depth, consistency, and reliability. Crafted for professionals worldwide who seek integrity, traceability, and performance.

Sustainable Farming & the Home of Our Cacao

Azeta sources single origin cacao from plantations located near the Anamalai Tiger Reserve region in Pollachi, Tamil Nadu. Our farming system follows pesticide free agricultural practices and promotes biodiversity through multi crop cultivation, including coconut, areca nut, and pepper.

Water use is managed through controlled drip irrigation. Cacao pods are harvested manually, followed by sun drying under natural conditions — ensuring traceability, environmental responsibility, and harmony with the surrounding ecosystem.

Cacao Farming, Fermentation & Drying

At Azeta, cacao farming, fermentation, and drying form the foundation of chocolate quality

1. Harvest

Mature cacao pods are carefully hand-harvested to ensure optimal ripeness and quality. Fresh beans are extracted on-site and prepared immediately, preserving their natural integrity and setting the foundation for consistent processing.

2. Fermentation

The beans are fermented using the traditional wooden box (teakwood) method for approximately six days. Native yeasts initiate the process, allowing natural acids and heat to develop gradually, breaking down cell structures and unlocking complex flavour precursors. Each batch is monitored daily to maintain ideal temperature, aeration, and bean health.

3. Sun Drying

Following fermentation, beans are sun dried under controlled conditions in Pollachi’s warm climate. This gradual reduction of moisture stabilizes flavour, prevents Mould development, and preserves aromatic compounds, ensuring the cacao is well prepared for further processing.

Our Tree-to-Bar Process

Sorting → Roasting → Cracking & Winnowing → Grinding → Tempering → Moulding & Packaging

Sorting

Once cacao beans arrive at our facility, they are carefully hand sorted and visually inspected. Beans showing signs of Mould, damage, or under fermentation are removed, ensuring that only the highest quality cacao moves forward.

Roasting

Sorted beans are gently roasted to unlock their full aromatic potential. This step deepens flavour, reduces bitterness, and develops the characteristic cocoa notes that define the final chocolate profile.

Cracking & Winnowing

Roasted beans are cracked and the outer husk is removed through winnowing, leaving behind clean cacao nibs, the pure essence of cacao that forms the foundation of fine chocolate.

Grinding & Conching

Cacao nibs are ground into a smooth cocoa liquor. Through refining and conching a controlled process of mixing, heat, and aeration, the chocolate develops a velvety texture while unwanted acidity and sharp notes are gently mellowed.

Tempering, Moulding & Packaging

The chocolate is precisely tempered through controlled heating and cooling to stabilize cocoa butter crystals, resulting in a glossy finish, clean snap, and stable structure. It is then moulded, carefully cooled, and hygienically packaged, ready for professional use or global export.
