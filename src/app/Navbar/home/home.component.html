What Makes Azeta Different?

Azeta originates in Pollachi, India, producing single origin cacao that is cultivated, fermented, and processed under close in house control. Using traditional wooden box fermentation alongside modern production systems. This integrated approach ensures traceability, consistency, and dependable performance across batches. Our chocolates unite craft integrity with industrial reliability. For global buyers seeking more than commodity cocoa, Azeta represents a refined, origin driven alternative.
